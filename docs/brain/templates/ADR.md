# ADR template

Copy into `decisions/ADR-NNN-short-title.md`, replace the prompts below, and link
it from the decision index. Follow [MEMORY-PROTOCOL.md](../MEMORY-PROTOCOL.md).

- **Decision ID:** unique ADR identifier.
- **Title:** concrete choice.
- **Status:** proposed / accepted / superseded, with approval evidence if accepted.
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Confidence and evidence baseline:** classification, revision and source.
- **Supersedes / superseded by:** related record or none.

## Context

Observed problem, scope and constraints; separate facts from hypotheses.

## Options considered

Real alternatives and tradeoffs; label retrospective interpretations explicitly.

## Decision

The chosen option, or the proposal if acceptance is pending.

## Rationale

Evidence and reasons for the choice.

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, risks, compatibility and any required follow-up verification.

## Related files and references

Relative source links, symbols, tests, revisions and related decisions/tasks.
